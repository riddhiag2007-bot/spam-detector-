      How it works

      The checklist adds points for red flags such as urgent wording, password requests, risky links, look-alike sender domains and risky attachments. The Naive Bayes model learned from 60 example messages how often each word appears in spam and in normal messages, and each word votes for one side. Both run in your browser, and nothing you type leaves this page. With so little training data this is a teaching demo, not a replacement for a real mail filter.
